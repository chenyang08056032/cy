# Doherty's special path

## Hingham captain shows
MIA way to college play

## By Mike Puzzanghera
And Nick Wolpert

Caroline Doherty is evidence that there’s no set path to a dream.

Hingham’s star forward — committed to play at Holy Cross after a post-grad year — has done it all while playing for the town she grew up in. She’s the school’s all-time leading scorer in two Patriot League MVP, and one of the state’s most talented players.

She takes immense pride in the career she's built at Hingham.

“Personally, I don’t think it matters where you go to school, as long as you put the work in,” Doherty said. “I get to play with my sister [Brenna] and all the people I grew up playing with. I think the public pride of it is honestly my favorite part.”

“I play in front of the little guys who all look up to all of us. That’s probably the best part of it, too.”

Doherty leads a Harborwomen team, ranked No. 3 in the United States Top 50, that has gone on to five title since 2011. They’re off to 6-0-0 and 4-0-0 with Doherty scoring 14 goals.

“She’s been playing every game, and she’s been these games and scored 17 goals,” third-year Hingham coach Courtney Turner said. “But she’s also been doing the little things that go into it, too: I think that says more about her character than her scoring.”

Doherty burst onto the scene as a freshman, then exploded for 48 goals and 28 assists as a sophomore. After another 36 points a year ago and 17 to start the new season, she's at 190 for her career. Of those, 126 are goals — the 122nd of which set the Hingham record on Christmas Day, assisted by her sister.
It’s easy to see the talent Doherty possesses. She can take possession of the puck, make a hard-hitting and snipe a corner back.

But it’s her work ethic and drive to be better that have gotten her here. Monday afternoon, she takes the ice near than 30 minutes before Hingham’s practice begins, getting into the flow of play and using it, said Andy Garity.

“She puts in a lot of work behind the scenes that allows her to be a goal scorer,” Turner said. “She also just goes to the gym all the time. Her drive to be a team member to how much work she puts in at the gym.”

Doherty has been named to the second team in the Massachusetts Hockey League, and she's displayed that talent and hard work — not the school or your money — make a college career possible.

“My first year, we had the prep school discussion,” Turner said. “She wanted to stay home and play soccer and I and how she’ll

have these friends for life."
Doherty made a switch at the club level this year, moving to the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, where she was named to the roster, but in the country. The move was all Doherty's, and she's taken every opportunity to build up her skill set to get an opportunity, and even though the Spitfires had a full roster, she impressed in her first two games. She finished with 10 points on the year over Labor Day weekend. Hingham will face Dover next week, and Doherty knows about how hard to look at.

Of the 19 players on the roster, she's the only one at an IMG program. Despite this, she finished the fall season as the team's top scorer with nine goals.
