
Vectors whose inner product is zero are called orthogonal. The length or norm of a vector in V is defined by

(2)

 \[\left\|\mathbf{a}\right\|=\sqrt{(\mathbf{a},\mathbf{a})}\qquad(\geq0).\] 

A vector of norm 1 is called a unit vector. From these axioms and from (2) one can derive the basic inequality

(3)

 \[\left\|\mathbf{a},\mathbf{b}\right\|\leq\left\|\mathbf{a}\right\|\left\|\mathbf{b}\right\|\] 

(Cauchy - Schwarz inequality)

From this follows

(4)

 \[\left\|\mathbf{a}+\mathbf{b}\right\|\leq\left\|\mathbf{a}\right\|+\left\|\mathbf{b}\right\|\] 

(Triangle inequality).

A simple direct calculation gives

(5)

 \[\left\|\mathbf{a}+\mathbf{b}\right\|^{2}+\left\|\mathbf{a}-\mathbf{b}\right\|^{2}=2(\left\|\mathbf{a}\right\|^{2}+\left\|\mathbf{b}\right\|^{2})\] 

(Parallelogram equality).
