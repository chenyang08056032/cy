7. 如图，梯形ABCD中，AB∥CD，若AB=1，CD=2，AC=6， \( S_{\Delta AOB}=1 \) ，则AD=___

 \( S_{\Delta AOB}=1 \) ，则AD=___


解：∵ AB∥CD

∴ △AOB∽△COD

即  \( \frac{AO}{CO} = \frac{AB}{CD} \)  ∵ AB = 1, CD = 2, AC = 6

∴  \( \frac{AO}{CO} = \frac{1}{2} \)  ⇒ CO = 2AO

又∵ AC = AO + CO = 3AO ⇒ 3AO = 6, AO = 2

又∵  \( \frac{S_{\Delta AOB}}{S_{\Delta COD}} = \left(\frac{AB}{CD}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{4} = \frac{1}{S_{\Delta COD}} \) 

∴  \( S_{\Delta COD} = 4 \) 

∴  \( S_{\Delta AOB} \)  与  \( S_{\Delta BOC} \)  (不同底, 同高)

且底之比为 OA : OC = 1 : 2

∴  \( S_{\Delta AOB} : S_{\Delta BOC} = 1 : 2 \)  ∴  \( S_{\Delta AOB} = 1 \) 

∴  \( S_{\Delta BOC} = 2 \) . 同理： \( S_{\Delta AOD} \)  与  \( S_{\Delta COD} \)  (不同底, 同高)

且底之比为 OA : OC = 1 : 2 ∴  \( S_{\Delta AOD} : S_{\Delta COD} = 1 : 2 \)
