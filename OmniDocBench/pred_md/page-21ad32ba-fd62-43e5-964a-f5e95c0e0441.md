
## SECTION A(1) (35 marks)

1. Simplify  \( (\alpha\beta^{3})(\alpha^{-2}\beta^{4})^{5} \)  and express your answer with positive indices. (3 marks)

 \[\begin{aligned}&\left(\alpha\beta^{3}\right)\left(\alpha^{-2}\beta^{4}\right)^{5}\\&=\left(\alpha\beta^{3}\right)\left(\alpha^{-10}\beta^{20}\right)\\&=\alpha\left(\beta^{3}\right)\left(\alpha^{-10}\right)\left(\beta^{20}\right)\\&=\alpha\left(\alpha^{-10}\right)\left(\beta^{3}\right)\left(\beta^{20}\right)\\&=\alpha^{-9}\beta^{23}\end{aligned}\] 

2. Make a the subject of the formula  \( \frac{4-3a}{b}=5 \) . (3 marks)

 \[\frac{4-3a}{b}=5\] 

 \[4-3a=5b\] 

 \[3a=\frac{4-5b}{4-5b}\]
