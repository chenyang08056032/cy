海伦公式:  \( S_{\Delta} = \sqrt{P(P-a)(P-b)(P-c)} \) 

(P为:  \( P = \frac{a+b+c}{2} \) , a, b, c为三角形边长)

那么:  \( S_{\Delta DQC} = \sqrt{\frac{11}{2} \cdot (\frac{11}{2} - 2) \cdot (\frac{11}{2} - 4) \cdot (\frac{11}{2} - 5)} \) 

 \( P = \frac{2 + 4 + 5}{2} = \frac{11}{2} \) 

 \( = \sqrt{\frac{11}{2} \cdot \frac{7}{2} \cdot \frac{3}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{2}} \) 

 \( = \frac{\sqrt{231}}{4} \) 

又:  \( S_{\Delta DQC} = \frac{1}{2} \times CQ \times DP \) 

 \( = \frac{1}{2} \times 2 \times DP = DP \) 

 \( \therefore DP = \frac{\sqrt{231}}{4} \)  根据①

 \( S_{\Delta 四边形 ABCD} = 9 \times \frac{1}{2} \times 9 \times DP = \frac{9\sqrt{231}}{4} \) 

根据(①) \( \frac{S_{\Delta mAD}}{S_{\Delta mAD} + \frac{9\sqrt{231}}{4}} = \frac{16}{25} \) 

 \( \Rightarrow S_{\Delta mAD} = 4\sqrt{231} \)  (end!)
