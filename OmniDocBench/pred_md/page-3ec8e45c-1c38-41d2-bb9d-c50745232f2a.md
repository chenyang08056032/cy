Completeness, Soundness and Entanglement lower bound: Here, we can assume that V is  \( \lambda \) -bounded. Thus, by Claim 4.49,  \( \mathcal{V}_{n}^{(1)} = \mathfrak{P}\mathfrak{a}\mathfrak{d}\mathfrak{o}\mathfrak{i}\mathfrak{n}(\mathcal{V}_{n}, n^{\lambda}) \) . Then, by Claim 4.50,  \( \mathcal{V}_{n}^{(2)} = \mathfrak{Q}\mathfrak{u}\mathfrak{e}\mathfrak{P}\mathfrak{e}\mathfrak{o}(\mathcal{V}_{2^{n}}^{(1)}, 2^{\lambda n}, \mathcal{B}(\lambda n)) \) . Finally, by Claim 4.46,  \( \mathcal{V}_{n}^{(3)} = \mathfrak{D}\mathfrak{e}\mathfrak{T}\mathfrak{i}\mathfrak{p}\mathfrak{e}(\mathcal{V}_{n}^{(2)}) \) . Now, as V is  \( \lambda \) -bounded,  \( \mathcal{V}' = (\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{QR}}^{\lambda}, \mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{QR}}^{\lambda}, \mathcal{L}', \mathcal{D}) \)  defines the same games as  \( \mathcal{V}^{(3)} \) , which means that

 \[\mathcal{V}_{n}^{\prime}=\mathfrak{D}\mathfrak{e}\mathfrak{T}\mathfrak{i}\mathfrak{p}\mathfrak{e}(\mathfrak{Q}\mathfrak{u}\mathfrak{e}\mathfrak{P}\mathfrak{e}\mathfrak{d}(\mathfrak{P}\mathfrak{a}\mathfrak{o}\mathfrak{d}\mathfrak{i}\mathfrak{n}(\mathcal{V}_{2^{n}},2^{\lambda n}),2^{\lambda n},\mathcal{B}(\lambda n))).\] 

By Fact 4.48, if  \( V_{2^{n}} \)  has a perfect ZPC strategy, then so does  \( \mathfrak{P}\mathfrak{a}\mathfrak{d}\mathfrak{o}\mathfrak{i}\mathfrak{n}(\mathcal{V}_{2^{n}},2^{\lambda n}) \) . Since V is  \( \lambda \) -bounded, the length of questions in the  \( 2^{n} \) -th game  \( V_{2^{n}} \)  (as well as in the padded version) is at most  \( (2^{n})^{\lambda}=2^{\lambda n} \) , which is the length of vectors returned by  \( \mathcal{B}(\lambda n) \) . Therefore, we may apply Theorem 4.24 to deduce that

 \[\mathfrak{Q}\mathfrak{u}\mathfrak{e}\mathfrak{P}\mathfrak{e}\mathfrak{d}(\mathfrak{P}\mathfrak{a}\mathfrak{o}\mathfrak{d}\mathfrak{i}\mathfrak{n}(\mathcal{V}_{2^{n}},2^{\lambda n}),2^{\lambda n},\mathcal{B}(\lambda n))\] 

has a perfect ZPC strategy as well. Finally, by Corollary 4.42, the detyping of the above has a perfect ZPC strategy, but this is exactly  \( V_{n}^{\prime} \) , proving the completeness requirements.

In the other direction, assume  \( \operatorname{val}^{*}(\mathcal{V}_{n}^{\prime})\geq1-\varepsilon \) . Then, as the typed graph of  \( \mathfrak{Q}\mathfrak{u}\mathfrak{e}\mathfrak{P}\mathfrak{e}\mathfrak{d} \) , described in Example 4.39 and in Figure 14, contains all self loops, using Corollary 4.43 we can deduce that the value of

 \[\mathfrak{Q}\mathfrak{u}\mathfrak{e}\mathfrak{P}\mathfrak{e}\mathfrak{d}(\mathfrak{P}\mathfrak{a}\mathfrak{o}\mathfrak{d}\mathfrak{i}\mathfrak{n}(\mathcal{V}_{2^{n}},2^{\lambda n}),2^{\lambda n},\mathcal{B}(\lambda n))\] 

is at least  \( 1 - O((2h + 28) \cdot 2^{4h + 56} \cdot \sqrt{\varepsilon}) = 1 - O_{h}(\sqrt{\varepsilon}) \) . Furthermore, for entanglement lower bound we have

 \[\mathcal{E}\left(\mathcal{V}_{n}^{\prime},1-\varepsilon\right)\geq\mathcal{E}\left(\mathfrak{Q}\mathfrak{u}\mathfrak{e}\mathfrak{P}\mathfrak{e}\mathfrak{d}\left(\mathfrak{P}\mathfrak{a}\mathfrak{o}\mathfrak{d}\mathfrak{i}\mathfrak{n}\left(\mathcal{V}_{2^{n}},2^{\lambda n}\right),2^{\lambda n},\mathcal{B}\left(\lambda n\right)\right),1-O_{h}\left(\sqrt{\varepsilon}\right)\right).\] 

By Theorem 4.24, if  \( \mathfrak{Q}\mathfrak{u}\mathfrak{e}\mathfrak{P}\mathfrak{e}\mathfrak{d}(\mathfrak{P}\mathfrak{a}\mathfrak{o}\mathfrak{d}\mathfrak{i}\mathfrak{n}(\mathcal{V}_{2^{n}},2^{\lambda n}),2^{\lambda n},\mathcal{B}(\lambda n)) \)  has value  \( 1 - O_{h}(\sqrt{\varepsilon}) \) , then  \( \mathfrak{P}\mathfrak{a}\mathfrak{o}\mathfrak{d}\mathfrak{i}\mathfrak{n}(\mathcal{V}_{2^{n}},2^{\lambda n}) \)  has value  \( 1 - O_{h}(h^{2}\cdot2^{h}\cdot(1+2^{2\lambda n}/d^{2})\cdot\varepsilon^{1/16}) = 1 - O_{h}(\varepsilon^{1/16}) \) , and

 \[1+2^{2\lambda n}/d^{2}\leq1+|\mathcal{B}|^{2}/d^{2}\leq1+\delta^{-2}=O(1),\] 

as d was the (un-normalized) distance of the error correcting code of dimension  \( 2^{\lambda n} \)  induced by  \( \mathcal{B} = \mathcal{B}(\lambda n) \) , and we chose B such that its distance is at least  \( \delta |B| \)  for a universal constant  \( \delta > 0 \)  (all of this was guaranteed by Fact 3.72). Furthermore, for entanglement lower bounds we have

 \[\mathcal{E}\left(\mathfrak{Q}\mathfrak{u}\mathfrak{e}\mathfrak{P}\mathfrak{e}\mathfrak{d}(\mathfrak{P}\mathfrak{a}\mathfrak{o}\mathfrak{d}\mathfrak{i}\mathfrak{n}\left(\mathcal{V}_{2^{n}},2^{\lambda n}\right),2^{\lambda n},\mathcal{B}(\lambda n)),1-O_{h}(\sqrt{\varepsilon})\right)\geq\mathcal{E}\left(\mathfrak{P}\mathfrak{a}\mathfrak{o}\mathfrak{d}\mathfrak{i}\mathfrak{n}\left(\mathcal{V}_{2^{n}},2^{\lambda n}\right),1-O_{h}(\varepsilon^{1/16})\right).\] 

Since V is  \( \lambda \) -bounded,  \( 2^{\lambda n} \)  is an upper bound on  \( |\mathcal{A}(2^{n},\cdot,\cdot)| \)  which is  \( \max\{\ell^{\mathfrak{P}^{\mathfrak{R}}},\ell^{\mathfrak{L}^{\mathfrak{R}}}\} \)  in  \( V_{2^{n}} \) . Hence, By Fact 4.48,  \( V_{2^{n}} \)  has value  \( 1-O_{h}(\varepsilon^{1/16}) \) , and

 \[\mathcal{E}\left(\mathfrak{P}\mathfrak{a}\mathfrak{o}\mathfrak{d}\mathfrak{i}\mathfrak{n}\left(\mathcal{V}_{2^{n}},2^{\lambda n}\right),1-O_{h}(\varepsilon^{1/16})\right)\geq\mathcal{E}\left(\mathcal{V}_{2^{n}},1-O_{h}(\varepsilon^{1/16})\right).\] 

Combining all of the above, gives the required soundness and entanglement lower bounds.

By choosing  \( c_{\mathrm{QR}}(h) \)  (121) to be large enough to bound all the constants along this proof, we conclude the theorem.
