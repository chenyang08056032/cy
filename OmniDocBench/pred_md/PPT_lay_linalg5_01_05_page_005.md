
## HOMOGENEOUS LINEAR SYSTEMS

■ Solve for the basic variables  \( x_{1} \)  and  \( x_{2} \)  to obtain  \( x_{1}=\frac{4}{3}x_{3} \) ,  \( x_{2}=0 \) , with  \( x_{3} \)  free.

■ As a vector, the general solution of Ax = 0 has the form given below.

 \[\mathbf{X}=\begin{bmatrix}x_{1}\\ x_{2}\\ x_{3}\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\frac{4}{3}x_{3}\\ 0\\ x_{3}\end{bmatrix}=x_{3}\begin{bmatrix}\frac{4}{3}\\ 0\\ 1\end{bmatrix}=x_{3}\mathbf{v},\ where\ \mathbf{v}=\begin{bmatrix}\frac{4}{3}\\ 0\\ 1\end{bmatrix}\]
