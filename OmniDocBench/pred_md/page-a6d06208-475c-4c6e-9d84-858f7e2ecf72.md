
Therefore, taking the expectation of both sides of the above equation over  \( \theta_{k+1} \)  conditioned on  \( F_{k} \) , we have

 \[\begin{aligned}&\mathbb{E}\left[\|C(X_{k+1})-Y_{k+1}\|^{2}\left|\mathcal{F}_{k}\right.\right]\\&=(1-b_{k})^{2}\|C(X_{k})-Y_{k}\|^{2}+\mathbb{E}\left[\|b_{k}I_{1,k}-I_{2,k}+I_{3,k}\|^{2}\left|\mathcal{F}_{k}\right.\right]\\&\quad+2(1-b_{k})\left\langle C(X_{k})-Y_{k},\mathbb{E}\left[b_{k}I_{1,k}-I_{2,k}+I_{3,k}\left|\mathcal{F}_{k}\right.\right]\right\rangle\\&\overset{(i)}{=}(1-b_{k})^{2}\|C(X_{k})-Y_{k}\|^{2}+b_{k}^{2}\mathbb{E}\left[\|I_{1,k}\|^{2}\left|\mathcal{F}_{k}\right.\right]+\mathbb{E}\left[\|I_{2,k}\|^{2}\left|\mathcal{F}_{k}\right.\right]+\|I_{3,k}\|^{2}-2b_{k}\mathbb{E}\left[\langle I_{1,k},I_{2,k}\rangle\left|\mathcal{F}_{k}\right.\right]\\&\quad+2b_{k}\left\langle\mathbb{E}\left[I_{1,k}\left|\mathcal{F}_{k}\right.\right],I_{3,k}\right\rangle-2\left\langle\mathbb{E}\left[I_{1,k}\left|\mathcal{F}_{k}\right.\right],I_{3,k}\right\rangle\\&\overset{(ii)}{=}(1-b_{k})^{2}\|C(X_{k})-Y_{k}\|^{2}+b_{k}^{2}\mathbb{E}\left[\|I_{1,k}\|^{2}\left|\mathcal{F}_{k}\right.\right]+\mathbb{E}\left[\|I_{2,k}\|^{2}\left|\mathcal{F}_{k}\right.\right]-\|I_{3,k}\|^{2}-2b_{k}\mathbb{E}\left[\langle I_{1,k},I_{2,k}\rangle\left|\mathcal{F}_{k}\right.\right]\\&\overset{(iii)}{\leq}(1-b_{k})^{2}\|C(X_{k})-Y_{k}\|^{2}+2b_{k}^{2}\mathbb{E}\left[\|I_{1,k}\|^{2}\left|\mathcal{F}_{k}\right.\right]+2\mathbb{E}\left[\|I_{2,k}\|^{2}\left|\mathcal{F}_{k}\right.\right]\\&\leq(1-b_{k})^{2}\|C(X_{k})-Y_{k}\|^{2}+2b_{k}^{2}\|X_{k}\|^{2}\mathbb{E}\left[\|M-M_{\theta_{k+1}}\|^{2}\left|\mathcal{F}_{k}\right.\right]+2\mathbb{E}\left[\|I_{2,k}\|^{2}\left|\mathcal{F}_{k}\right.\right]\\&\overset{(iv)}{\leq}(1-b_{k})^{2}\|C(X_{k})-Y_{k}\|^{2}+2b_{k}^{2}\tau_{x}^{4}v_{m}^{2}+4\tau_{x}^{2}\tau_{m}^{2}\|X_{k+1}-X_{k}\|^{2},\end{aligned}\] 

where the equations  \( (i) \)  and  \( (ii) \)  use the facts that  \( E[I_{1,k}|\mathcal{F}_{k}]=0 \)  and  \( E[I_{2,k}|\mathcal{F}_{k}]=E[I_{3,k}|\mathcal{F}_{k}]=0 \) , the inequality  \( (iii) \)  follows from the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality  \( -2b_{k}\left\langle I_{2,k},I_{3,k}\right\rangle\leq b_{k}^{2}\|I_{1,k}\|^{2}+\|I_{2,k}\|^{2} \) , and the inequality  \( (iv) \)  leverages the result in (42). The proof is completed. ☐
