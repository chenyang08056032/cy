
例：求单位脉冲函数 \( \delta(t) \) 的拉氏变换。



 \[\delta(t)=\lim_{t_{0}\to0}\frac{1(t)-1(t-t_{0})}{t_{0}}\] 

 \[\begin{aligned}L[\delta(t)]&=\int_{0}^{\infty}\delta(t)e^{-st}dt=\int_{0}^{\infty}\lim_{t_{0}\to0}\frac{1(t)-1(t-t_{0})}{t_{0}}e^{-st}dt\\&=\lim_{t_{0}\to0}\left[\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{e^{-st}}{t_{0}}dt-\frac{1(t-t_{0})e^{-s(t-t_{0})}}{t_{0}}e^{-st_{0}}dt\right]=\lim_{t_{0}\to0}\frac{1-e^{-t_{0}s}}{t_{0}s}=1\end{aligned}\]
