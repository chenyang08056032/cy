
Assume that M halts. Let N be the number of time steps it takes M to halt. For every  \( n \geq N \) , by Corollary 2.60,  \( V_{n}^{M,\lambda} \)  has a perfect ZPC strategy. So, if  \( C \geq N \) , then we are done. Otherwise, let n be such that

 \[\max(C,\log N)\leq n<N.\] 

By Lemma 2.59,  \( \mathcal{V}_{n}^{\mathcal{M},\lambda} = (\mathcal{V}^{\mathcal{M},\lambda})_{n}^{\prime} \)  in this case. By the compression theorem 2.53, since  \( n \geq C \) ,  \( (\mathcal{V}^{\mathcal{M},\lambda})_{n}^{\prime} \)  has a perfect ZPC strategy given that  \( V_{2^{n}}^{\mathcal{M},\lambda} \)  has one. But  \( 2^{n} \geq N \) , and we already argued that these tailored games have perfect ZPC strategies. Hence,  \( V_{n}^{\mathcal{M},\lambda} \)  has a perfect ZPC strategy when  \( n \geq \max(C, \log N) \) . If  \( C \geq \log N \) , then we are done. Otherwise, we can iterate this argument and deduce the same for any  \( n \geq \max(C, \log \log N) \) . Since there exists some t for which  \( C \geq \underbrace{\log \ldots \log}_{t- \text{times}} N \) , we deduce that  \( \mathfrak{G}_{\mathcal{M}} = \mathcal{V}_{\mathcal{C}}^{\mathcal{M},\lambda} \)  has a perfect ZPC strategy. This proves (2) in Theorem 2.31.

t-times

Assume that M does not halt. Then, by Lemma 2.59,

 \[\mathcal{V}_{n}^{\mathcal{M},\lambda}=(\mathcal{V}^{\mathcal{M},\lambda})_{n}^{\prime}\] 

for every \(n\). If \(n \geq C\), then by the compression theorem 2.53, we have

 \[\mathcal{E}\big((\mathcal{V}^{\mathcal{M},\lambda})_{n^{\prime}}^{\prime},1/2\big)\geq\mathcal{E}(\mathcal{V}_{2^{n}}^{\mathcal{M},\lambda},1/2)\quad\mathrm{a n d}\quad\mathcal{E}\big((\mathcal{V}^{\mathcal{M},\lambda})_{n^{\prime}}^{\prime},1/2\big)\geq\underbrace{2^{2\lambda n}-1}_{\geq2^{n}}.\] 

So, for every positive integer t we can deduce that

 \[\begin{aligned}\mathcal{E}\big(\mathcal{V}_{\mathcal{C}}^{\mathcal{M},\lambda},1/2\big)=&(11)\mathcal{E}\big((\mathcal{V}^{\mathcal{M},\lambda}\big)_{\mathcal{C}}^{\prime},1/2)\\ \geq&(12)\mathcal{E}\big(\mathcal{V}_{\mathcal{2}^{\mathcal{C}}}^{\mathcal{M},\lambda},1/2\big)=&(11)\mathcal{E}\big((\mathcal{V}^{\mathcal{M},\lambda}\big)_{\mathcal{2}^{\mathcal{C}}}^{\prime},1/2)\\ &\vdots\\ \geq&(12)\mathcal{E}\big(\mathcal{V}_{\mathcal{2}^{\mathcal{C}}}^{\mathcal{M},\lambda},1/2\big)=&(11)\mathcal{E}\big((\mathcal{V}^{\mathcal{M},\lambda}\big)^{\prime}\underbrace{2^{\mathcal{C}}}_{\text{t-times}},\underbrace{2^{\mathcal{C}}}_{\text{t-times}}\\ &\geq(12)\underbrace{2^{\mathcal{C}}}_{\text{(t+1)-times}}.\end{aligned}\] 

Since this was true for every t, we can deduce that  \( \mathcal{E}(\mathcal{V}_{C}^{\mathcal{M},\lambda},1/2)=\infty \) , which in turn proves that

 \[\mathrm{v a l}^{*}(\mathfrak{G}_{\mathcal{M}})=\mathrm{v a l}^{*}(\mathcal{V}_{\mathcal{C}}^{\mathcal{M},\lambda})<1/2,\] 

proving (3) in Theorem 2.31.
