## 7 Complete the sentences with the correct form of the verbs in brackets.

1 If you ___ (not understand), ___ (ask) your teacher.

2 She ___ (not be) satisfied unless her son ___ (win) first place.

3 If you ___ (see) a red light, ___ (not cross) the road.

4 You ___ (not feel) relaxed unless you ___ (learn) to let go.

1 don't understand, ask 2 won't be, wins 3 see, don't cross 4 won't feel, learn
