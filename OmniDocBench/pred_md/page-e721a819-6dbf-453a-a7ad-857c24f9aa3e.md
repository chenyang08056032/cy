
5.1. Expansion in the q-aspect. Since  \( \pi_{1} \)  and  \( \pi_{2} \)  are cuspidal automorphic representations, We note that  \( \Phi_{1} = \varphi_{1}\varphi_{2}^{q} \)  is a rapid-decay function which is invariant under the congruence group  \( \mathbf{K}_{0}(\mathfrak{w}\mathfrak{q}) \) , we apply Plancherel formula (See [Zac20, Theorem 2.8]) to the well-defined inner product (5.2) in the space of forms of level mq. We have the following decomposition of the considered period

(5.3)

 \[\mathcal{P}_{\mathfrak{q}}(\mathfrak{l},\Phi,\Phi)=\mathcal{G}_{\mathfrak{q}}(\mathfrak{l},\Phi,\Phi)+\mathcal{C}_{1},\] 

where the generic part is given by

(5.4)

 \[\begin{align*}\mathcal{G}_{\mathfrak{q}}(\mathfrak{l},\Phi,\Phi)&=\sum_{\substack{\pi\mathrm{ cuspidal}\\ \mathfrak{c}(\pi)\mathrm{l u u q}}}\lambda_{\pi}(\mathfrak{l})\sum_{\psi\in\mathcal{B}(\pi,\mathfrak{u u q}}}\left|\langle\varphi_{1}\varphi_{2}^{\mathfrak{q}},\psi\rangle\right|^{2}\\&\quad+\sum_{\substack{\chi\in F^{\times}\backslash\mathbb{A}_{F}^{1}\\ \mathfrak{c}(\chi)^{2}\mathrm{l u u q}}}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\lambda_{\chi,it}(\mathfrak{l})\sum_{\psi_{it}\in\mathcal{B}(\chi,\chi^{-1},it,\mathfrak{u u q})}\left|\langle\varphi_{1}\varphi_{2}^{\mathfrak{q}},\mathrm{E}(\psi_{it})\rangle\right|^{2}\frac{dt}{4\pi}.\end{align*}\] 

Moreover, the constant term  \( C_{1} \)  is the one-dimensional contribution (constant term) which appears only if both  \( \pi_{1} \)  and  \( \pi_{2} \)  are cuspidal and there exists finite many quadratic characters  \( \chi \)  (depending on the number field F) of  \( F^{\times}\backslashA_{F}^{1} \)  such that  \( \pi_{1}\simeq\pi_{2}\otimes\chi \)  ( \( \chi=1 \)  if  \( \pi_{1}=\pi_{2} \)  for example). The cardinality of such quadratic character  \( \chi \)  is  \( O_{\varepsilon,F}((uvq)^{\varepsilon})=O_{\varepsilon,F}(q^{\varepsilon}) \) . For such a quadratic character  \( \chi \) , by applying the Hecke relation (3.5), we have the following:

(5.5)

 \[\begin{align*}\mathcal{C}_{1}&=V_{F}^{-1}\cdot\langle\mathrm{T}_{\mathrm{I}}(\Phi),\varphi_{\chi}\rangle\langle\varphi_{\chi},\Phi\rangle=\frac{\chi(\mathfrak{l})\deg(\mathrm{T}_{\mathrm{I}})}{V_{F}}\cdot\left|\langle\varphi_{1}\varphi_{2}^{\mathfrak{q}},\varphi_{\chi}\rangle\right|^{2}\\&=\frac{\zeta_{\mathfrak{q}}(2)^{2}\chi(\mathfrak{l})\deg(\mathrm{T}_{\mathrm{I}})}{\zeta_{\mathfrak{q}}(1)^{2}V_{F}}\cdot\left|\frac{1}{q^{1/2}}\left(\langle\varphi_{1}\cdot(\mathrm{T}_{\mathfrak{q}}\varphi_{2}),\varphi_{\chi}\rangle-\frac{1}{q_{1}}\cdot\langle\varphi_{1}\cdot(\mathrm{T}_{\mathfrak{q}_{1}^{m-2}}\varphi_{2}),\varphi_{\chi}\rangle\right)\right|^{2}\\&=\frac{\zeta_{\mathfrak{q}}(2)^{2}\chi(\mathfrak{l})\deg(\mathrm{T}_{\mathrm{I}})}{\zeta_{\mathfrak{q}}(1)^{2}V_{F}}\cdot\left|\frac{1}{q^{1/2}}\left(\lambda_{\pi_{2}}(\mathfrak{q})-\frac{1}{q_{1}}\lambda_{\pi_{2}}\left(\mathfrak{q}_{1}^{m-2}\right)\right)\cdot\langle\varphi_{1}\varphi_{2},\varphi_{\chi}\rangle\right|^{2}\\&=\frac{\zeta_{\mathfrak{q}}(2)^{2}\chi(\mathfrak{l})\deg(\mathrm{T}_{\mathrm{I}})}{q\zeta_{\mathfrak{q}}(1)^{2}V_{F}}\cdot\left|\left(\lambda_{\pi_{2}}(\mathfrak{q})-\frac{1}{q_{1}}\lambda_{\pi_{2}}\left(\mathfrak{q}_{1}^{m-2}\right)\right)\right|^{2}\cdot\left|\langle\varphi_{1}\varphi_{2},\varphi_{\chi}\rangle\right|^{2},\end{align*}\] 

where  \( T_{q} \)  is the Hecke operator and  \( \zeta_{\mathfrak{q}}(s):=\prod_{l\mid\mathfrak{q}}\zeta_{F_{v}}(s) \)  is the partial Dedekind zeta function. If the integral ideal q is squarefree, i.e. m=1, we define  \( T_{\mathfrak{q}_{1}^{m-2}}:=0 \)  and  \( \lambda_{\pi_{2}}\left(\mathfrak{q}_{1}^{m-2}\right):=0 \) . Hence,  \( T_{q_{1}^{m-2}}\varphi_{2}=0 \) . Moreover, the degree of the Hecke operator  \( T_{l} \)  is defined by

(5.6)

 \[\deg(\mathrm{T}_{\mathrm{I}}):=\frac{1}{\ell^{1/2}}\sum_{0\leqslant k\leqslant\frac{n}{2}}\gamma_{n-2k}=\ell^{1/2}\frac{\zeta_{F_{\mathfrak{p}}}(1)}{\zeta_{F_{\mathfrak{p}}}(n+1)}\leqslant\ell^{1/2}\zeta_{F_{\mathfrak{p}}}(1).\] 

Since  \( \pi_{2} \)  is  \( \theta_{2} \) -tempered at all the finite places, we have  \( |\lambda_{\pi_{2}}(\mathfrak{q})|\leqslant(m+1)\cdot q^{\theta_{2}} \) . Applying Cauchy-Schwartz inequality,

 \[\mathcal{C}_{1}\ll_{\pi_{1},\pi_{2},F,\varepsilon}(\ell q)^{\varepsilon}\frac{\ell^{1/2}}{q^{1-2\theta_{2}}}\ll_{\pi_{1},\pi_{2},F,\varepsilon}(\ell q)^{\varepsilon}\frac{\ell^{1/2}}{q^{25/32}}\] 

since  \( 0 \leqslant \theta_{2} \leqslant \frac{7}{64} \) . Hence we can conclude

(5.7)

 \[\mathcal{P}_{\mathfrak{q}}(\mathfrak{l},\Phi,\Phi)=\mathcal{G}_{\mathfrak{q}}(\mathfrak{l},\Phi,\Phi)+O_{\pi_{1},\pi_{2},F,\varepsilon}\left((\ell q)^{\varepsilon}\frac{\ell^{1/2}}{q^{1-2\theta_{2}}}\right),\] 

where we recall that  \( \Phi \)  is defined as (5.1).
