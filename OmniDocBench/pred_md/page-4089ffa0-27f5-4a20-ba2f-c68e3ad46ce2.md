We now show that we can add the integral with  \( R_{n+1} \)  and  \( R_{n+2} \)  to the error term  \( \tilde{R}_{n+1}(t) \) . Using that  \( \frac{1}{2}\log t \leq \log(t/u) \leq \log t \)  for  \( 1 \leq u \leq \sqrt{t} \) , we get for  \( n \geq 1 \)

Similarly for the integral with  \( R_{n+2} \)  and also for n = 0. This completes the proof of (6.47) and that  \( g(t) \)  is a strange function.
