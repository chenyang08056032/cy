
(c) Hom-Leibniz conformal identity:

 \[\begin{aligned}&[\alpha(p)_{\lambda}[q_{\mu}r]_{\mathcal{N}_{h}}]_{\mathcal{N}_{h}}\\=&[\alpha(p)_{\lambda}([\mathcal{N}_{h}(q)_{\lambda}r]+[q_{\lambda}\mathcal{N}_{h}(r)]-\mathcal{N}_{h}[q_{\lambda}r])]_{\mathcal{N}_{h}}\\=&[\alpha(p)_{\lambda}[\mathcal{N}_{h}(q)_{\mu}r]]_{\mathcal{N}_{h}}+[\alpha(p)_{\lambda}[q_{\mu}\mathcal{N}_{h}(r)]]_{\mathcal{N}_{h}}-[\alpha(p)_{\lambda}\mathcal{N}_{h}[q_{\mu}r]]_{\mathcal{N}_{h}}\\=&[\mathcal{N}_{h}(\alpha(p))_{\lambda}[\mathcal{N}_{h}(q)_{\mu}r]]+[\alpha(p)_{\lambda}\mathcal{N}_{h}([\mathcal{N}_{h}(q)_{\mu}r])]-\mathcal{N}_{h}([\alpha(p)_{\lambda}[\mathcal{N}_{h}(q)_{\mu}r]])\\&+[\mathcal{N}_{h}\alpha(p)_{\lambda}[q_{\mu}\mathcal{N}_{h}(r)]]+[\alpha(p)_{\lambda}\mathcal{N}_{h}([q_{\mu}\mathcal{N}_{h}(r)])]-\mathcal{N}_{h}([\alpha(p)_{\lambda}[q_{\mu}\mathcal{N}_{h}(r)])]\\&-[\mathcal{N}_{h}\alpha(p)_{\lambda}\mathcal{N}_{h}([q_{\mu}r])]-[\alpha(p)_{\lambda}\mathcal{N}_{h}^{2}([q_{\mu}r])]+\mathcal{N}_{h}([\alpha(p)_{\lambda}\mathcal{N}_{h}([q_{\mu}r])])\\=&[\mathcal{N}_{h}(\alpha(p))_{\lambda}[\mathcal{N}_{h}(q)_{\mu}r]]+[\alpha(p)_{\lambda}([\mathcal{N}_{h}(q)_{\mu}\mathcal{N}_{h}(r)])]-\mathcal{N}_{h}([\alpha(p)_{\lambda}[\mathcal{N}_{h}(q)_{\mu}r]])\\&+[\mathcal{N}_{h}\alpha(p)_{\lambda}[q_{\mu}\mathcal{N}_{h}(r)]]-\mathcal{N}_{h}([\alpha(p)_{\lambda}[q_{\mu}\mathcal{N}_{h}(r)])]\\&-[\mathcal{N}_{h}\alpha(p)_{\lambda}\mathcal{N}_{h}([q_{\mu}r])]+\mathcal{N}_{h}([\alpha(p)_{\lambda}\mathcal{N}_{h}([q_{\mu}r])]).\end{aligned}\] 

Similarly, we have

 \[\begin{aligned}&[[p_{\lambda}q]_{\mathcal{N}_{h}\lambda+\mu}\alpha(r)]_{\mathcal{N}_{h}}\\=&[[\mathcal{N}_{h}(p)_{\lambda}\mathcal{N}_{h}(q)]_{\lambda+\mu}\alpha(r)]+[[p_{\lambda}\mathcal{N}_{h}(q)]_{\mathcal{N}_{h}\lambda+\mu}\mathcal{N}_{h}(\alpha(r))]-\mathcal{N}_{h}[[p_{\lambda}\mathcal{N}_{h}(q)]_{\lambda+\mu}\alpha(r)]\\&+[[\mathcal{N}_{h}(p)_{\lambda}q]_{\lambda+\mu}\mathcal{N}_{h}(\alpha(r))]-\mathcal{N}_{h}[[\mathcal{N}_{h}(p)_{\lambda}q]_{\lambda+\mu}\mathcal{N}_{h}\alpha(r)]\\&-[\mathcal{N}_{h}[p_{\lambda}q]_{\lambda+\mu}\mathcal{N}_{h}(\alpha(r))]+\mathcal{N}_{h}[\mathcal{N}_{h}[p_{\lambda}q]_{\lambda+\mu}\alpha(r)],\end{aligned}\] 

and

 \[\begin{aligned}&[\alpha(q)_{\mu}[p_{\lambda}r]_{\mathcal{N}_{h}}]_{\mathcal{N}_{h}}\\=&[\mathcal{N}_{h}(\alpha(q))_{\mu}[\mathcal{N}_{h}(p)_{\lambda}r]]+[\alpha(q)_{\mu}([\mathcal{N}_{h}(p)_{\lambda}\mathcal{N}_{h}(r)])]-\mathcal{N}_{h}([\alpha(q)_{\mu}[\mathcal{N}_{h}(p)_{\lambda}r]])\\&+[\mathcal{N}_{h}\alpha(q)_{\mu}[p_{\lambda}\mathcal{N}_{h}(r)]]-\mathcal{N}_{h}([\alpha(q)_{\mu}[p_{\lambda}\mathcal{N}_{h}(r)])]\\&-[\mathcal{N}_{h}\alpha(q)_{\mu}\mathcal{N}_{h}([p_{\lambda}r])]+\mathcal{N}_{h}([\alpha(q)_{\mu}\mathcal{N}_{h}([p_{\lambda}r])]).\end{aligned}\] 

From the above equations we conclude that Hom-Leibniz conformal identity holds.

Hence  \( (\mathcal{L}_{\mathcal{N}_{h}}, [\cdot_{\lambda}]\mathcal{N}_{h}, \alpha) \)  is a Hom-Leibniz conformal algebra.

(2) For any  \( p, q \in L \) , we have

 \[\begin{align*}&[\mathcal{N}_{h}(p)_{\lambda}\mathcal{N}_{h}(q)]_{\mathcal{N}_{h}}\\=&[\mathcal{N}_{h}(\mathcal{N}_{h}(p))_{\lambda}\mathcal{N}_{h}(q)]+[\mathcal{N}_{h}(p)_{\lambda}\mathcal{N}_{h}(\mathcal{N}_{h}(q))]-\mathcal{N}_{h}([\mathcal{N}_{h}(p)_{\lambda}\mathcal{N}_{h}(q)])\\=&\mathcal{N}_{h}([\mathcal{N}_{h}(\mathcal{N}_{h}(p))_{\lambda}q]+[\mathcal{N}_{h}(p)_{\lambda}\mathcal{N}_{h}(q)]-\mathcal{N}_{h}([\mathcal{N}_{h}(p)_{\lambda}q]))\\&+\mathcal{N}_{h}([\mathcal{N}_{h}(p)_{\lambda}\mathcal{N}_{h}(q)]+[p_{\lambda}\mathcal{N}_{h}(\mathcal{N}_{h}(q))]-\mathcal{N}_{h}([p_{\lambda}\mathcal{N}_{h}(q)]))\\&-\mathcal{N}_{h}^{2}([\mathcal{N}_{h}(p)_{\lambda}q]+[p_{\lambda}\mathcal{N}_{h}(q)]-\mathcal{N}_{h}([p_{\lambda}q]))\\=&\mathcal{N}_{h}([\mathcal{N}_{h}(p)_{\lambda}q]_{\mathcal{N}_{h}}+[p_{\lambda}\mathcal{N}_{h}(q)]_{\mathcal{N}_{h}}-\mathcal{N}_{h}([p_{\lambda}q]_{\mathcal{N}_{h}})).\end{align*}\] 

Hence, \(\mathcal{N}_{h}\) is also a Nijenhuis operator on the Hom-Leibniz conformal algebra \((\mathcal{L}_{\mathcal{N}_{h}}, [\cdot_{\lambda}]\mathcal{N}_{h})\).

(3) It is obvious to observe that the map  \( \mathcal{N}_{h}:(\mathcal{L}_{\mathcal{N}_{h}},[\cdot_{\lambda}]\mathcal{N}_{h},\alpha)\to(\mathcal{L}_{\mathcal{N}_{h}},[\cdot_{\lambda}],\alpha) \) ,  \( p\mapsto\mathcal{N}_{h}(p) \)  is a morphism of Hom-Nijenhuis Leibniz conformal algebras.

It completes the proof.

The following proposition describes the close relationship between the Nijenhuis operator and the Rota-Baxter operator. Similar results hold for Hom-associative conformal algebras (see prop 6.2 of [4]). Let us observe, if these results hold for Hom-Leibniz conformal algebras or not.

Proposition 2.10. Let  \( N_{h}:L\toL \)  be a C[ \( \partial \) ]-module homomorphism over a Hom-Leibniz conformal algebra L. Then

(1)  \( N_{h} \)  is a Nijenhuis operator if and only if  \( N_{h} \)  is a Rota-Baxter-operator of weight 0, given that  \( N_{h}^{2}=0 \) .

(2)  \( N_{h} \)  is a Nijenhuis operator if and only if  \( N_{h} \)  is a Rota-Baxter-operator of weight -1, given that  \( N_{h}^{2}=N_{h} \) .

(3)  \( N_{h} \)  is a Nijenhuis operator if and only if  \( N_{h} \)  is a modified RB-operator of weight  \( \mp1 \) , given that  \( N_{h}^{2} = \pm Id \) .

(4)  \( N_{h} \)  is a Nijenhuis operator if and only if  \( N_{h} \pm Id \)  is a RB-operator of weight  \( \mp2 \) , given that  \( N_{h}^{2} = Id \) .
