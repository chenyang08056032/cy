
Upper bounding  \( \gamma_{2} \)  in (S.21). Using (S.9) in Lemma S.5 and (S.28), we have with probability at least  \( 1 - O(d^{-10}) \)  that

 \[\begin{align*}\gamma_{2}&=\max_{i\in[N]}\left\|\mathbf{E}_{i,:}\left(\mathbf{V}\mathbf{V}^{\top}\mathbf{V}^{*}-\mathbf{V}^{(-i)}\mathbf{V}^{(-i)\top}\mathbf{V}^{*}\right)\right\|\\&\lesssim\max_{i\in[N]}\left\|\mathbf{E}_{i,:}\right\|\left\|\mathbf{V}\mathbf{V}^{\top}-\mathbf{V}^{(-i)}\mathbf{V}^{(-i)\top}\right\|\\&\lesssim\sigma\sqrt{J}\max_{i\in[N]}\left\|\mathbf{V}\mathbf{V}^{\top}-\mathbf{V}^{(-i)}\mathbf{V}^{(-i)\top}\right\|\\&\lesssim\frac{\sigma\sqrt{J}}{\sigma_{K}^{*}}\left(\widetilde{\sigma}\sqrt{K\log d}+MB\log d\left\|\mathbf{V}^{*}\right\|_{2,\infty}+\sigma\sqrt{J}\left\|\mathbf{U}^{*}\right\|_{2,\infty}+\right.\\&\left.\quad\sigma\sqrt{J}\left\|\mathbf{U}\mathbf{U}^{\top}\mathbf{U}^{*}-\mathbf{U}^{*}\right\|_{2,\infty}+\gamma_{1}\right).\end{align*}\] 

Combining (S.16) with the upper bounds for  \( \gamma_{1} \)  and  \( \gamma_{2} \) , with  \( \sigma\sqrt{J}=o(\sigma_{K}^{*}) \) , we have with probability at least  \( 1-O(d^{-10}) \)  that
