
## Example 1

(a) If  \(  f(x) = xe^{x}  \) , find  \(  f'(x)  \) .

(b) Find the nth derivative,  \( f^{(n)}(x) \) .

Solution:

(a) By the Product Rule, we have

 \[f^{\prime}(x)=\frac{d}{dx}\left(xe^{x}\right)\] 

 \[=x\frac{d}{dx}\left(e^{x}\right)+e^{x}\frac{d}{dx}\left(x\right)\] 

 \[=xe^{x}+e^{x}\cdot1=(x+1)e^{x}\]
