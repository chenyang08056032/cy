
Proof. Let us first verify (121). For any  \( 2k + l \leqslant 2m \) ,  \( l \geqslant 1 \) , we apply (134) with  \( K = k + \frac{l}{2} \)  and  \( L = l + 1 \) , and using that the neutral term is bounded,

 \[\mathrm{h}_{q-2k-l}^{k,l}\lesssim\sum_{i=1}^{N}\left(\int_{\Omega\times\mathbb{R}^{n}}|D_{x}^{k+\frac{l}{2}}f|^{2}\left\langle v\right\rangle^{q-2k-l}\mathrm{d}v\mathrm{d}x\right)^{\theta_{i}^{\prime}}\left(\int_{\Omega\times\mathbb{R}^{n}}\sum_{l^{\prime}\leqslant L}|\partial_{x}^{l+1}f|^{2}\left\langle v\right\rangle^{q-2k-l}\mathrm{d}v\mathrm{d}x\right)^{\theta_{i}^{\prime\prime}},\] 

with  \( \theta_{i}^{\prime}+\theta_{i}^{\prime\prime}<1 \) . So, if l is even, then the x-integral on the right hand side consists of integer order derivatives and therefore,

(123)

 \[\mathrm{h}_{q-2k-l}^{k,l}\lesssim\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathrm{x}^{\theta_{i}^{\prime}}\mathrm{d}^{\theta_{i}^{\prime\prime}}.\] 

If l is odd,  \( l = 2p + 1 \) , and hence  \( k + p + 1 \leqslant m \) , we use interpolation in x and distribute the weight accordingly,

 \[\begin{align*}\int_{\Omega\times\mathbb{R}^{n}}|D_{x}^{k+p+\frac{1}{2}}f|^{2}\left\langle v\right\rangle^{q-2k-l}\mathrm{d}v\mathrm{d}x&\leqslant\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\left(\int_{\Omega}|\partial_{x}^{k+p+1}f|^{2}\mathrm{d}x\right)^{1/2}\left(\int_{\Omega}|\partial_{x}^{k+p}f|^{2}\mathrm{d}x\right)^{1/2}\mathrm{d}v\\=&\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\left(\int_{\Omega}|\partial_{x}^{k+p+1}f|^{2}\left\langle v\right\rangle^{q-2k-2p-2}\mathrm{d}x\right)^{1/2}\left(\int_{\Omega}|\partial_{x}^{k+p}f|^{2}\left\langle v\right\rangle^{q-2k-2p}\mathrm{d}x\right)^{1/2}\mathrm{d}v\\\leqslant&\left(\int_{\Omega\times\mathbb{R}^{n}}|\partial_{x}^{k+p+1}f|^{2}\left\langle v\right\rangle^{q-2k-2p-2}\mathrm{d}v\mathrm{d}x\right)^{1/2}\left(\int_{\Omega\times\mathbb{R}^{n}}|\partial_{x}^{k+p}f|^{2}\left\langle v\right\rangle^{q-2k-2p}\mathrm{d}v\mathrm{d}x\right)^{1/2}\end{align*}\] 

So, if k = p = 0, then the above is bounded by  \( \lesssim x^{1/2} \) , and if  \( k + p > 0 \) , then by  \( \lesssim x \) . In either case we arrive at the same bound (123).

Summing up over  \( 2k + l \leqslant 2m \) ,  \( l \geqslant 1 \) , and reindexing the exponents, we obtain

 \[\mathrm{v}\lesssim\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathrm{x}^{\theta_{i}^{\prime}}\mathrm{d}^{\theta_{i}^{\prime\prime}},\] 

with N depending only on the indexes.

As to the mixed term, we have

 \[\int_{\Omega\times\mathbb{R}^{n}}\left\langle v\right\rangle^{q-2k}\partial_{x}^{k}f\cdot\partial_{x}^{k-1}\partial_{v}f\mathrm{d}v\mathrm{d}x\leqslant\left(\int_{\Omega\times\mathbb{R}^{n}}|\partial_{x}^{k}f|^{2}\left\langle v\right\rangle^{q-2k}\mathrm{d}v\mathrm{d}x\right)^{1/2}\left(\int_{\Omega\times\mathbb{R}^{n}}|\partial_{x}^{k-1}\partial_{v}f|^{2}\left\langle v\right\rangle^{q-2k}\mathrm{d}v\mathrm{d}x\right)^{1/2}\] 

using (134) with K = k, L = 2k,

 \[\leqslant\mathrm{h}^{1/2}\sum_{i}(\mathrm{h}_{q-2k}^{k,0})^{\theta_{i}^{\prime}/2}(\mathrm{h}_{q-2k}^{0,2k})^{\theta_{i}^{\prime\prime}/2}\leqslant\sum_{i}\mathrm{h}^{\theta_{i}},\] 

where all  \( \theta_{i} < 1 \) . After adding up the terms and relabeling the exponents, we obtain

 \[\mathrm{m}\lesssim\sum_{i}\mathrm{h}^{\theta_{i}}.\]
